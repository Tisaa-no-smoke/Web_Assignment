រឿងរបស់យើង · Our Story

Made in Cambodia, for Cambodian homes

A small studio in Phnom Penh, working with artisan villages across the Kingdom

Kingdom Homecraft began on Street 240 in Daun Penh with a simple belief: a Cambodian home deserves Cambodian craft. We travel between Phnom Penh, Kampong Cham, Takeo, Pursat, and Mondulkiri to work directly with weavers, woodworkers, ceramicists, and farmers.

Every rattan chair, every krama-wrapped cushion, every Kampot pepper candle is made by people we know by name. We pay fair prices, we ship across the Kingdom, and we keep our prices honest — both in USD and in riel.

Khmer artisans first

40+ partner workshops across Cambodia, paid fairly and credited by name.

Local materials

Rattan, teak, silk, water hyacinth, ceramic — all sourced inside the Kingdom.

Delivered with care

Same-week delivery in Phnom Penh, 2 or 3 days to the provinces, by trusted couriers.
